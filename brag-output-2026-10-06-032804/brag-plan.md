# Brag plan — "La tercera opción" (TikTok, español)

## Rubric
1. **What:** An ad for Raymundo's service: he makes video ads for businesses.
2. **For:** Small businesses and creators who need ads for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
3. **Tension:** Cheap sellers deliver ugly ads that don't sell. Expensive agencies charge a lot, take weeks, and deliver average work.
4. **Claim:** A third option: professional ads at a fair price.
5. **Proof:** The video itself is the sample: "Sí, este anuncio también lo hice yo."
6. **Tone:** polished × cinematic, Spanish, with direct TikTok copy.
