# Brag plan — "The Third Option"

## Planning rubric

1. **What is it?** A personal ad for Raymundo, an independent builder who ships apps and AI products end to end: phone apps, web apps, backends, and AI integrations.
2. **Who is it for?** Anyone who needs an app or AI product built and has been burned by, or is afraid of, the two usual options.
3. **The tension.** Cheap freelancers deliver broken work. Expensive agencies charge a fortune for mediocre results. Buyers think those are the only choices.
4. **The claim.** There is a third option: fair price, real results, shipped live.
5. **Proof from the code.** The work in this repo shows the range: a Flutter phone app, a native Kotlin Android Auto screen, a serverless backend, and three AI models (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT) wired in and selectable. It's deployed and live. The video uses this as proof of capability without being a car-app video.
6. **Tone.** `polished` × `cinematic`. A serious, confident, premium trailer. No jokes, no jingle.
7. **Hook.** Name the buyer's pain in the first second: "The cheap developer." → crossed-out result.
8. **Visual identity.** Warm near-black (#0C0B0A), ivory type (#F1ECE2), one brass accent (#D4A24C). Instrument Serif for statements (human, crafted), JetBrains Mono for labels and data (engineering). Slow camera drift, hairline rules, faint grain-free vignette glow.
9. **CTA.** "Message me. Let's build it." with the name lockup.

## Format
Landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, 23.0s.

## Music cue guidance
No bundled music track and no catalog access in this environment, so the bed is a custom-synthesized cinematic drone (low D pedal, detuned low-passed pad, and an open fifth that brightens after the reveal). It's deliberately non-melodic, so it reads as a premium trailer bed and not a jingle. Cue points: 0.0 enter, 6.6 drop, 9.2 open, 19.2 swell, 22.0–23.0 fade. SFX come from the bundled Pixabay-licensed library: bass impacts, riser, whooshes, and a soft click.
