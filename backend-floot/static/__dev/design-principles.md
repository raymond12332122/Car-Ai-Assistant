Distinctive thread: night cockpit instrument. The UI reads like a premium car's digital cluster at night: deep graphite ground, glassy instrument surfaces, one luminous cyan signal that means "the assistant is listening / act here", and nothing else competing for the eye. Every choice serves a glance taken at 100 km/h.

Mode: dark-first. A light palette exists for daytime phone use, but the car surfaces always force dark.

Pacing: generous and coarse. Touch targets are at least 64px tall (Android Auto / driver distraction guidance), primary actions 96px+. Text never below 18px on driving screens; replies are short and set large. Max two levels of hierarchy per screen.

Surfaces: matte graphite cards with a 1px hairline border and subtle inner glow, large radii (20-28px) like molded dashboard trim. Shadows are soft and deep, never crisp. The cyan signal is used only for the mic button, listening state, and the single primary action on a card (Navigate). Warnings (low fuel, errors) use amber, never red unless critical.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque for display (speedometer-like character, tight tabular numerals); Inter Tight for body. Numbers (distance, ETA) use tabular figures.

Motion: slow, calm pulses for listening; no bouncy or attention-seeking animation while driving.
